excerpt: >-
  High-dimensional nonlinear operators are common in modern learning and scientific workloads, yet efficient implementation under tight area and energy budgets remains challenging. Stochastic computing (SC) offers compact logic via Bernoulli bitstreams, but its practical use is limited by correlation-induced multiplication bias, unstable large-fan-in accumulation, and the exponential cost of direct multivariate tabulation. In this paper, we propose STSCA (Scalable Tensor-Train Stochastic Computing with Stabilized Arithmetic), which combines an FSM-generated codeword with a tensor-train parameter tensor for streaming M-variate nonlinear implementation without exponential lookup-table (LUT) growth, and integrates correlation-robust multiplication with scalable accumulation using a trainable dynamic scaling factor to stabilize reduction-heavy computations. On M = 16 benchmarks, STSCA achieves low error with short bitstreams, shows modest rank sensitivity, and consistently benefits from decorrelation and trainable scaling over fixed scaling or no scaling.
