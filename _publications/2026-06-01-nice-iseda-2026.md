title: "NICE: 3D-NAND-based In-Memory-Computing with In-Situ ECC-Protection for Fault-Tolerant and Efficient LLM Inference"

excerpt: >-
  Large language models (LLMs) demand massive weight storage, making edge deployment challenging. 3D NAND Flash offers high density for hosting LLM weights, and analog compute-in-memory (CIM) can improve throughput by reducing data movement and enabling multi-wordline parallelism. However, analog NAND-CIM reliability is limited by raw bit error rate (RBER) and read-out variation, while conventional NAND ECC and prior CIM-oriented codes are not directly compatible with in-situ MAC pipelines. We propose NICE, an ECC-protected analog 3D NAND-CIM architecture that interleaves parity/check-bit columns along the page direction and computes syndromes in parallel with MAC outputs, enabling on-die correction without reducing CIM parallelism. On HellaSwag with Qwen3-30B-A3B (W8A4, 6-bit ADC), NICE reduces codeword FER by 66.4%/34.9% versus No-ECC/single error correction at the highest error point, improving accuracy to 62.0% from 24.7% and 51.9%. End-to-end, NICE incurs only 0.7% throughput loss versus CIM without ECC (7.63 to 7.58 tokens/s) while achieving a 4.59x speedup over a von Neumann baseline.
