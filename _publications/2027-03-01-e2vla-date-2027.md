title: "E2VLA: Accelerating Vision-Language-Action Models Inference on FPGA with Early Exiting"

excerpt: >-
  Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models enable end-to-end robotic control but are too compute- and memory-intensive for resource-constrained FPGA platforms. Early exiting reduces computation by halting inference when consecutive layers yield similar action predictions, yet on FPGAs, it suffers from repeated action-head evaluations, dependency stalls between exit checks and the backbone, and poor resource sharing between the vision and language stages. We present E2VLA, an algorithm-architecture co-design that overcomes these issues with three coupled techniques: a speculative single-dimension proxy that triggers full-action verification only when necessary, an asynchronous bypass action-head engine that overlaps exit checks with backbone execution, and a decoupled compute-and-accumulation mechanism that shares GEMM resources across vision and language stages. On an AMD VHK158 FPGA, E2VLA achieves 2.23x speedup and 3.34x higher energy efficiency on OpenVLA-OFT, and 2.45x/4.27x on OpenVLA, over GPU baselines, while retaining 96.2% average success on LIBERO on OpenVLA-OPT (only 0.9 points below the OpenVLA-OFT baseline).
