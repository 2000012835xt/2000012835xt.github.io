excerpt: >-
  Embodied Artificial Intelligence (AI) has recently attracted significant attention as it bridges AI with the physical world. It consists of two sequentially executed stages, perception stage implemented with ResNet and policy stage with transformer-based diffusion model. It necessitates an efficient unified accelerator to maximize hardware utilization and lower overall cost when deployed at edge. Stochastic computing (SC), a novel computation paradigm, represents a number by a bitstream in which the probability of 1's denotes its value. Compared to the binary representation, this representation enables SC to simplify the arithmetic circuits. Besides, the fault tolerance from encoding makes SC work at near threshold voltage and emerged as a promising solution for efficient inference.
