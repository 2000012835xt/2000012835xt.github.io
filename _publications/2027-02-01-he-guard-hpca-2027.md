title: "HE-Guard: Efficient Fault Recovery for Reliable Homomorphic Encryption Accelerators"

excerpt: >-
  Fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) has emerged as a promising paradigm for privacy-preserving machine learning (PPML). However, FHE incurs significant computation cost and often requires large-scale accelerators implemented in advanced technology nodes. As these accelerators scale, they face growing reliability challenges arising from variation, aging, noise, etc. In FHE, even a single bit flip can catastrophically corrupt the entire plaintext information encoded in a ciphertext, causing unacceptable accuracy degradation in PPML inference. Existing protection techniques, however, are insufficient: on one hand, they still suffer from high accuracy degradation due to limited error detection coverage and imbalanced protection across the FHE pipeline; on the other hand, they incur prohibitive error recovery overhead for highly pipelined FHE accelerators. To address these challenges, we propose HE-Guard, an end-to-end reliable FHE architecture with high-coverage detection and low-overhead recovery. HE-Guard co-designs rigorous error detection algorithms and topology-adaptive hardware to achieve high-coverage and timely error detection. We further develop a dataflow-aware architecture that aligns error recovery granularity with computation parallelism, achieving uniform protection across different FHE primitives with minimum recovery overhead. We integrate HE-Guard into representative FHE accelerators and synthesize them with a 12nm library. Compared to prior schemes, HE-Guard can maintain the accuracy of PPML inference even with a 3 to 6 orders of magnitude higher error rate. Meanwhile, HE-Guard reduces recovery latency overhead by up to 927x and by 319x on geometric mean, while only incurring 1.5% area overhead as well as 2.8% power overhead.
